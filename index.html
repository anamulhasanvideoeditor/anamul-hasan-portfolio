A little about me

Good stories deserve great editing.

I’m Anamul Hasan, a video editor and motion designer with 3+ years of experience. I enjoy turning raw footage into clear, engaging stories—whether that's a YouTube video, a brand promotion, a travel film or short-form social content.

My focus is on strong storytelling, thoughtful pacing, clean visuals and sound that supports the message. I work to understand each project's goal and shape the edit around its audience.
